🚀 Mission Context (VSSC / ISRO)

M250 Maraging Steel (18% Ni, 8% Co, 5% Mo, 0.4% Ti) is deployed in critical aerospace launch vehicle thin-shell booster structures joined by TIG welding (LSLF: Longitudinal Seam Launch and Finish). Repair cycles degrade joint properties due to thermal cycles and precipitate dissolution. Guaranteeing minimum R2 properties non-destructively is vital for flight clearance.

🧠 Deep Learning Architectures

The platform incorporates 4 specialized neural architectures:
• 1D-CNN Regressor: Captures localized feature correlations.
• BiLSTM Regressor: Bidirectional recurrence over sequential feature representations.
• CNN+LSTM Hybrid: Combines convolution feature extraction with temporal cell state.
• Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN): Constrains predictions to adhere to physical yield/UTS ratios.

🛡️ Monte Carlo Dropout Uncertainty

For mission-critical aerospace deployment, point estimates are insufficient. We implement Monte Carlo Dropout with 50 forward passes per inference, yielding rigorous 95% confidence intervals (±2σ) and establishing conservative Minimum Guaranteed R2 Properties that aerospace engineers can trust for safety certification.
